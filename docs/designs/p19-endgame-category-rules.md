# P19 Representative Endgame Category Rules (US-004)

## In-scope categories (first closure set)

| Kind | Label | Trajectory emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `legendary_echo` | 传奇余响 | High achievement + legacy transmission + admired public arc |
| `bittersweet_closure` | 有成有憾 | Strong stats with unmet maintenance, feud, or burden |
| `quiet_continuity` | 烟火延续 | Family/local focus; modest Jianghu footprint |
| `isolated_fade` | 孤影渐隐 | Low connections, withdrawn life states, weak faction ties |
| `infamous_echo` | 争议回响 | High evil_karma, feud exposure, or disputed hero mantle |

## Trajectory inputs (selection / weighting)

- **Relationship score** — allies, disciples, spouse, feud count, betrayal flags
- **Faction score** — sect duty, protection vs exposure patterns, organizational standing
- **Legacy score** — P18 succession quality, transmission outcomes, inheritance channels
- **Achievement score** — reputation, martialPower, hero maintenance satisfaction
- **Burden score** — sworn enemies, unmet obligation, inherited burden outcomes

Selection: weighted candidate list from active `endgameCategoryConfigs`; highest weight wins. Age is a weak tie-breaker only (not primary driver).
