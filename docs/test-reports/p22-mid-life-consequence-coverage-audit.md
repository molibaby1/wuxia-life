# P22 Mid-Life Consequence Coverage Audit (US-002)

Read-only audit of relationship consequence, faction consequence, and social-role continuation pools.

## Summary

Mid-life consequence infrastructure exists (P17 patterns) but sustained-role content remains thin for mentor obligations, sect continuation, and merchant identity maintenance.

## Pool Assessment

| Pool | Classification | Main risk |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Relationship consequence (`love.json`, P17 patterns) | **weak** | mentor_bond and ally_network flags rarely trigger dedicated mid-life obligation events |
| Faction consequence (`faction-revelation.json`) | **moderate** | sect_exposure sustained beyond age 30 is sparse |
| Social-role continuation (`middle-age-career.json`) | **weak** | merchant and social-connector roles lack identity-maintenance beats |

## Player-Experience Risks

1. **Relationship fade** — early mentor/ally ties stop mattering after growth phase.
2. **Faction discontinuity** — joined_sect identity does not sustain into mid-life duty events.
3. **Route differentiation decay** — mid-life runs converge on generic career/conflict events.

## Non-Goals

No gameplay behavior changes in this audit story.
