## Strong Support Reasons (martial / balanced)

- P8 martial + balanced personas have explicit training goals and route reinforcement.
- P16 martial-family origin surfaces bias training tags early.
- P11 route scheduling boosts active-route events by 1.35×.
- P9 replay pair comparison shows martial-lin vs cautious-han collapse on `summary_identity` and `active_action_distribution`.

## Weak / Missing Reasons Summary

1. **Config gaps** — No unified archetype-family scoring across lifecycle inputs; each phase (P16–P19) resolves independently without cross-stage archetype contract.
2. **Over-dominant routes** — Martial and balanced paths absorb scheduling multipliers; scholar/wealth/social lack compensating pacing offsets.
3. **Pool scarcity** — Early childhood/youth events share `martial_talent_acknowledged` summary templates across unlike origins.
4. **Pacing collapse** — P9 baseline: all 8 personas show 6–7y low-impact spans; midlife and late-life density homogenizes before legacy/endgame layers activate.
