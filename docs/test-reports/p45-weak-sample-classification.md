## Bucket Analysis

### 1. Content coverage thin

Current evidence: **secondary contributor, not primary**

Why:

- P44 shows `businessHabit` has only 4 total readers and partial differentiation
- P44 explicitly flags `businessHabit` as single-cluster dominated

Implication:

- livelihood replay weakness is not just bad luck
- the content surface for business-led reinforcement is thinner than training or study

### 2. Trigger density too low

Current evidence: **possible contributor**

Why:

- business and mixed trajectories still show few visible midlife consequence signals in the compact replay output
- mixed has no visible consequence signal by 30 or 40

Implication:

- even when early preference exists, the replay may not receive enough reinforcing or redirecting signals later

### 3. Shaping signal too weak

Current evidence: **primary contributor**

Why:

- all four baseline personas still show training-led top-axis outcomes by age 20
- scholar, business, and mixed now pick up some secondary non-training accumulation, but intended pushes still do not win the early dominant-axis race

Implication:

- the shaping mechanism is present
- but non-training pushes are still not accumulating strongly enough, early enough, in whole-life replay

### 4. Callback too late or too weak

Current evidence: **primary contributor for scholar**

Why:

- scholar-specific callbacks appear by age 30 and 40
- but they arrive after the dominant shaping line has already settled as training-led

Implication:

- downstream echoes exist
- they are not strong enough to reshape trajectory-level identity

### 5. Route differentiation collapse

Current evidence: **primary contributor for business and mixed**

Why:

- business diverges in route label, but not into the intended livelihood identity
- mixed no longer ends as a pure single-axis collapse, but still fails to retain a mixed top-axis profile

Implication:

- visible route variance is not yet translating into intended life-direction variance

## Non-Bucketed Findings
