**Internal reports only (no structured playtest capture)**

## Main calibration gaps

1. **No playtest feedback schema** — human judgments are not machine-readable alongside internal gates.
2. **RC decisions implicit** — release readiness depends on maintainer knowledge, not explicit RC evaluation surface.
3. **Internal-external misalignment invisible** — P23 can pass while first-run readability or ending aftertaste still fail in playtest.
4. **No RC false-positive sample** — strong internal health with weak outward appeal is not systematically demonstrated.
5. **Playtest dimensions scattered** — first-run, onboarding, replay, route, payoff, and ending lack a unified calibration model.

## P24 targets

- Unify playtest dimensions under explicit calibration baselines wired through profile-first reporting.
- Add playtest feedback, RC evaluation, and internal-external alignment comparison surfaces.
- Prove bounded RC wave can redirect fixes when internal metrics miss player-facing problems.
