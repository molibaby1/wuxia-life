# P92 Medical Endgame Direction — GO/NO-GO Assessment

> **Date:** 2026-06-29
> **Stage:** P92 Wuxia Medical Endgame Design-First Contract
> **Purpose:** Assess whether medical endgame is worth doing, and if so, define its core positioning
> **Target Route:** `medical_sage_healer` (一代名医)
> **Origin:** `tavern_hand` (酒肆帮工)
> **Variants:** Compassionate (仁心医者) + Pragmatic (世故人医)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL_GO — but with strict lightweight constraints**

Endgame adds meaningful narrative value for the medical route because the core theme of medical is "医者" (healer). The transition from "活人的医者" (healer of the living) to "身后的医名" (posthumous legacy of healing) is a natural culmination of the medical theme — something that late-life doesn't fully address.

The medical route has a unique advantage over renown: **2 variants × 3 choices = 6 branches**, which allows for richer endgame differentiation. The compassionate and pragmatic variants can explore fundamentally different endgame axes:
- **Compassionate endgame:** 仁心之传承 — your spirit of compassion lives on through the people you saved and students you taught
- **Pragmatic endgame:** 医名之流传 — your medical reputation and social position echo through history

However, this is a **conditional GO**:
- Must stay lightweight: 1 echo event + expression updates only
- Must have 6 meaningfully different variants (one per late-life branch)
- Must clearly differentiate from both late-life AND renown endgame AND generic P19 endgame
- Must preserve tavern-born medical healer flavor
- 2 variants must feel fundamentally different (not mirrored)

If any of these conditions can't be met, it should be NO-GO.

## 2. Late-Life Closure Assessment

### 2.1 What Late-Life Already Provides

Late-life (P91, age 52-56) already delivers:
- ✅ 6 distinct branches with clear identities (2 variants × 3 choices)
- ✅ Strong narrative closure for each path
- ✅ Stat changes that reflect late-life state
- ✅ 5 expression surfaces updated × 6 branches = 30 updates
- ✅ Tavern-born flavor consistent throughout
- ✅ Complete narrative arc: origin → bridge → entry → on-ramp → pressure → payoff → late-life
- ✅ 2 variants clearly differentiated (body/spirit vs social/position)

### 2.2 What Late-Life Does NOT Address

Late-life is still an **active life stage** — the character is still "living their life as a healer." What it doesn't provide:

1. **Legacy perspective:** From "being a healer" to "what remains of your healing"
2. **Third-person memory angle:** The medical route is about healing people — but late-life is still about *your* experience of healing, not about *others' memory* of your healing
3. **Final coda feel:** Late-life feels like the last chapter of an active life; endgame should feel like the epilogue
4. **Variant-specific culmination:**
   - Compassionate: From "我在救人" to "我的仁心传下去了"
   - Pragmatic: From "我有名医地位" to "我的医名流传后世"
5. **"From healer to legacy" transition:** This is the natural thematic peak of the medical route

### 2.3 Closure Sufficiency Verdict

**Late-life provides strong closure, but not final closure for the medical theme specifically.**

For some routes, late-life might be sufficient. But for medical route — whose entire identity is built around healing and the impact of a healer's life — the transition from "living healer" to "healing legacy" is the natural thematic peak.

Medical has an additional advantage over renown: **2 variants × 3 choices = 6 branches**, which means endgame can explore two fundamentally different axes of legacy (spiritual/ethical vs social/reputational), making it richer and more differentiated.

## 3. Endgame Core Positioning

### 3.1 Selected Direction: Medical Legacy Echo (医名身后事)

**Endgame = "世人如何记住这位医者" 的最终回声**

Not life review. Not death scene. Not new choices.

Instead: a single echo event at age 60-65 where you realize — or hear — how the world now remembers you as a healer. Your medical legacy has outgrown you; it's become something people talk about, pass on, remember.

This is the natural culmination of the medical theme:
- Bridge: 你开始行医
- Entry: 医者之路分岔（仁心 vs 世故）
- On-ramp: 医名初起
- Pressure: 仁心之累 / 世故之秤
- Payoff: 仁心之解 / 世故之解
- Late-life: 你晚年如何做医者
- **Endgame: 世人如何记住这位医者**

### 3.2 Two-Variant Axis Differentiation

The 2-variant structure allows for fundamentally different endgame directions:

| Axis | Compassionate Endgame | Pragmatic Endgame |
|------|----------------------|-------------------|
| **Core question** | 你的仁心，传下去了吗？ | 你的医名，流传下来了吗？ |
| **Legacy type** | Spiritual / ethical legacy | Social / historical legacy |
| **Focus** | People you saved, students you taught, compassion that lives on | Your reputation, your status, your medical school of thought |
| **Tone** | Warm / bittersweet / inspiring | Grand / nostalgic / complex |
| **Analogy** | 薪火相传 — 火种不灭 | 医名远播 — 名声流传 |
| **Memory carrier** | Patients' lives, students' practice, the spirit of healing | Jianghu stories, medical records, social standing |

**This is the key differentiator from renown endgame:** Renown has a single axis (jianghu reputation). Medical has two axes (spiritual legacy vs social legacy), making it inherently richer and more differentiated.

### 3.3 Why This Direction

| Reason | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| **Thematic fit** | Medical is about healing and impact; legacy echo is a healer's impact's final form |
| **Differentiates from late-life** | Late-life = your experience of being an old healer; Endgame = others' memory of your healing |
| **Differentiates from renown endgame** | Renown = jianghu reputation / social network legacy; Medical = healing legacy with two axes (spiritual + social) |
| **Differentiates from P19 generic endgame** | P19 = comprehensive life review + historical memory; Medical endgame = focused on healing/medical legacy only |
| **Lightweight compatible** | Can be done as 1 echo event + expression updates |
| **6-branch leverage** | Each late-life branch naturally leads to a different "how they remember you as a healer" |
| **2-variant leverage** | Compassionate vs pragmatic explore fundamentally different legacy axes |
| **Tavern-born flavor** | 酒肆里的药香、老掌柜的教导、从酒肆走出来的医者 — all tavern-born |

### 3.4 What Endgame Is NOT

- ❌ Not a new life stage with new goals
- ❌ Not a death scene
- ❌ Not a choice event (player already made all their choices)
- ❌ Not a multi-event arc
- ❌ Not a generic "回顾一生"
- ❌ Not a renown endgame reskin (medical has 2 variants, different legacy axes)

### 3.5 Essential Difference: Endgame vs Late-Life

| Dimension | Late-Life (52-56) | Endgame (60-65) |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| **Perspective** | First-person (your life as a healer) | Third-person (world's memory of your healing) |
| **Core theme** | 你晚年如何做医者 | 世人如何记住这位医者 |
| **Agency** | You're still practicing / living it | It's happening around you / to your legacy |
| **Tone** | Earned rest / consequence | Echo / memory / legacy |
| **Compassionate axis** | Body/spirit — how aging affects your healing | Spiritual legacy — how your compassion lives on |
| **Pragmatic axis** | Social/position — your status in late life | Social legacy — how your name/reputation echoes |
| **Event type** | Auto (consequence) | Auto (echo / realization) |

## 4. Six Branches — Unified or Differentiated?

### 4.1 Decision: 6 Distinct Endgame Variants

**Yes, all six late-life branches need distinct endgame echoes.**

Rationale:
1. Each late-life branch has a fundamentally different identity
2. Each naturally leads to a different "how the world remembers you as a healer"
3. The 6-branch / 2-variant structure is what makes medical route unique — endgame should deepen that, not flatten it
4. Lightweight constraint is still satisfied (1 event with 6 variants, not 6 separate events)
5. Both variants have fundamentally different legacy axes (spiritual vs social), so they won't feel like mirrors

## 5. Endgame vs Renown Endgame vs Generic P19 Endgame — Distinction

### 5.1 Medical Endgame vs Renown Endgame

| Dimension | Medical Endgame (P92/93) | Renown Endgame (P80/81) |
|-----------|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| **Branches** | 6 (2 variants × 3 choices) | 3 (1 variant × 3 choices) |
| **Variant structure** | 2 fundamentally different variants (compassionate + pragmatic) | Single variant (jianghu renown) |
| **Legacy axes** | 2 axes: spiritual/healing legacy + social/medical legacy | 1 axis: jianghu reputation legacy |
| **Core identity** | Healer / doctor | Jianghu networker / mediator |
| **Flavor anchors** | 药庐、病人、徒弟、医术、仁心、世故 | 酒肆、后辈、江湖传说、人情 |
| **Core question** | 世人如何记住这位医者？ | 江湖如何记住你？ |
| **Compassionate tone** | Spiritual, warm, healing-focused | N/A |
| **Pragmatic tone** | Social, complex, reputation-focused | Same as single axis |

**Key distinction:** Medical endgame has **two fundamentally different legacy axes** (spiritual healing vs social reputation) because of the 2-variant structure. Renown endgame has only one axis (jianghu reputation). This makes medical endgame inherently richer and more complex.

### 5.2 Medical Endgame vs Generic P19 Endgame

| Dimension | Medical Endgame (P92/93) | Generic P19 Endgame |
|-----------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| **Scope** | Route-specific (medical only) | All routes / all origins |
| **Focus** | Healing / medical legacy specifically | Comprehensive: relationships + factions + legacy + memory |
| **Timing** | 60-65 (echo event) | End of life / death |
| **Depth** | Single focused echo with 6 variants | Multi-faceted recovery + memory |
| **Flavor** | Tavern-born medical healer specific | Generic wuxia |
| **Purpose** | Culmination of medical theme | Final life closure |

**Key distinction:** Medical endgame is a **route-specific thematic coda**, not a generic end-of-life system. It deepens the medical route's core theme (healing / medical legacy).

### 6.3 Final Verdict

**CONDITIONAL_GO**

**Why GO:**
- Thematic fit is strong — healer → healing legacy is the natural medical culmination
- 6-branch structure creates meaningful differentiation (2× renown's complexity)
- 2 variants have fundamentally different legacy axes (spiritual/healing vs social/medical) — not mirrored
- Can be done within lightweight constraint
- Tavern-born flavor enhances well (酒肆药香、老掌柜的传承 angle)
- Late-life provides life closure but not thematic closure
- Clearly differentiated from renown endgame (2 variants vs 1, healing vs reputation)
- Adds value beyond P19 generic endgame (route-specific, 6 variants, healing focus)

**Why conditional (not full GO):**
- Narrative value is real but modest — it's a coda, not a new act
- 6 branches means more implementation work than renown (but still lightweight)
- Single origin (tavern_hand) means lower replication value
- Must strictly adhere to lightweight constraint
- If implementation starts creeping beyond 1 event + expression, stop and reconsider
- Must ensure 2 variants don't end up feeling like mirrors of each other
