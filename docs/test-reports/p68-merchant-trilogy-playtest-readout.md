### 1.2 Review Protocol

For each route, the reviewer:
1. Reads the full payoff-phase expression (current goal + age-40 identity + cost label)
2. Answers 5 standard questions (per P46 §10.2 checklist, adapted)
3. Writes a one-sentence retell of the route
4. Rates the route pass/warning/fail

Then, after all three:
1. 30-second retell test — can the retell all three routes distinctly?
2. Cross-route comparison — do they feel like different lives?

## 5. Cross-Route Retell Test (30秒复述)

### 5.1 One-Sentence Per Route

Prompt: 30秒内，用一句话分别复述三条路线的区别。

| Route | One-Sentence Retell | Distinguishable? |
|-------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Apprentice** | "手艺人靠品质眼光做成商人，代价是失去手艺自由。" | ✅ Yes — 刨子/账本/算 |
| **Tavern** | "开酒馆的靠人情网络做成商人，代价是欠人人情。" | ✅ Yes — 酒肆/商号/织 |
| **Peasant** | "庄稼人靠脚力血汗做成粮商，代价是回不到田里。" | ✅ Yes — 田埂/车马/踩 |

### 5.2 Can You Tell Them Apart?

**Answer:** Yes, clearly.

The three routes feel like three different lives:

1. **Apprentice** — "The Calculator" — success through quality and judgment; cost is losing craft freedom
2. **Tavern** — "The Weaver" — success through connections and favors; cost is social debt
3. **Peasant** — "The Stepper" — success through labor and endurance; cost is leaving the land

The distinctive verbs help a lot: **算出 / 织出 / 踩出** — three different kinds of action, three different kinds of success.

### 5.3 Which Feels Most Different?

**Peasant** — "从田埂到车马" has the strongest physical imagery and the clearest "before/after" contrast. The "回不到田里了" hits hardest emotionally.

**Tavern** is the most conceptually interesting — "人情债" (favor debt) is a unique cost that neither of the others have.

**Apprentice** is the most relatable — the "craftsman becomes manager" arc is familiar but well-executed.

## 8. What Worked

1. **出身呼应做得好的地方：**
   - "从X到Y"的结构 — 画面感强，前后对比清晰
   - 三个独特动词（算/织/踩）— 动作感不一样，成功形状就不一样
   - 代价和成功是一体两面 — 你付的价正好对应你成功的方式
   - 命运句格式统一但内容不同 — 好记也好区分

2. **最有记忆点的三个画面：**
   - 刨子 → 账本（学徒）
   - 人情比银子多（酒肆）
   - 回不到田里（农家）

3. **玩家能记住差异。** 不是"三条商路的三个变体"，是三种不同的成功人生。

## 9. Caveats and Warnings

1. **纯文字层面的区别 — 没有机制层面的区别。** 所有区别都在文字表达里，不在玩法机制上。三条路线还是一样的骨架。
2. **命运句没有接入UI —** 函数存在但玩家不一定能看到。如果不在结局或总结页面展示。
3. **只有一个评审者 —** 样本量小，是内部评审，不是外部用户测试。
4. **基于表达审查，不是全程游玩 —** 是看表达文本，不是实际玩游戏。

这些是已知限制，不是失败。
