# P23 Measurable vs Inferred Comparison Surfaces Audit

Read-only audit of where experience comparison is explicit, partial, or inferred only.

## Classification matrix

| Experience surface | Measurable | Partially measurable | Inferred only |
|--------------------|------------|----------------------|---------------|
| Archetype emergence | Family match score | Arc distinctiveness beyond route label | Arc "texture" |
| Replay novelty | Repeat decay, pool diversity | Cross-run histogram delta | "Freshness" feel |
| Route differentiation | Route point scheduling, path affinity | Pacing multiplier delta | Narrative voice per route |
| Stage pacing | Density multipliers, payoff spacing | Callback cadence years | Rhythm "breathing room" |
| Mid/late payoff | Consequence pattern engagement | Payoff spacing scores | Consequence emotional weight |
| Legacy resonance | Legacy shape flags, successor signals | Transmission vs rupture ratio | Dynasty/story resonance |
| Endgame aftertaste | Endgame category, memory tone | Recovery pattern match | Closing prose impact |

## Gates that can pass without clear experience improvement

| Gate | What it proves | What it does not prove |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| gate:playability | 8 personas survive to 40 with readable flow | Late-life payoff, legacy, endgame quality |
| gate:p20 | Archetype families emerge; pacing differs | Player would choose to replay for quality |
| gate:p21 | Content samples pass constraints | Wave improved targeted experience dimension |
| gate:p22 | Pools meet minimums; weak spots flagged | Weak spots became player-facing improvements |

## Content-volume-without-quality risks

1. Adding events to a pool raises `eventCount` without raising `archetypeSupportScore`.
2. Tuning `baseWeight` without acceptance baseline may shift distribution without improving weak slices.
3. Expansion waves can pass workflow steps while comparison shows flat experience deltas.
4. LLM-assisted content can pass style constraints but fail replay distinctiveness.

## Weakest evidence boundaries (P23 priority)

1. **Whole-life vs slice** — existing slices are strong per phase but lack unified acceptance scorecard.
2. **Wave comparison** — P22 tuning compares metrics; P23 must tie metrics to experience dimensions.
3. **Low-value wave detection** — no single report says "content added, experience flat."
4. **Tuning redirection** — decisions are documented in samples but not enforced through acceptance workflow.

## Deferred beyond P23

- Fully automated subjective prose quality scoring
- Real-player telemetry and A/B testing infrastructure
- UI-facing acceptance dashboards
