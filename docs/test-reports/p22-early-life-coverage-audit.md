# P22 Early-Life Content Library Coverage Audit (US-001)

Read-only audit of origin, childhood shaping, and early route-divergence pools before P22 expansion.

## Summary

Early-life pools have improved since P16 but remain uneven: martial and balanced trajectories dominate; frontier, streetborn, and wealth merchant early forks stay thin.

## Pool Assessment

| Pool | Classification | Main risk |
|------|----------------|-----------|
| Origin (`origin.json` + `WUXIA_ORIGIN_SURFACES`) | **moderate** | frontier_military and streetborn lack dedicated milestone events beyond trait flags |
| Childhood shaping (`general.json` age 0–12) | **weak** | formative choices not strongly conditioned on origin shaping tendencies |
| Early route divergence (`p9-remediation`, `p21-content-samples`) | **sparse** | wealth merchant and explorer-style early reinforcement underrepresented |

## Player-Experience Risks

1. **Origin collapse** — frontier and streetborn runs feel like reskinned poor/martial starts.
2. **Childhood homogenization** — training loop dominates formative years regardless of shaping profile.
3. **Early route thinness** — wealth and social-connector trajectories lack distinct age-18–24 fork events.

## Non-Goals

No gameplay behavior changes in this audit story.
