# P67 Success-Shape and Recap Strength Audit

> **Date:** 2026-06-28
> **Stage:** P67 Wuxia Merchant Trilogy Success Shape And Recap
> **Story:** P67-001 — Audit current success-shape and recap strength
> **Type:** Audit — documentation only, no runtime changes

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This audit inventories the existing success-shape and recap signals across the three merchant bridge routes (apprentice, tavern, peasant) and identifies where success feels generic versus distinct, where the recap/retrospective layer is strong versus thin, and what the highest-leverage spots are for P67 to target.

**Core finding:** After P66, the trilogy has strong **cost differentiation** and **entry differentiation**, but the **success shape** still reads as "same success, different flavor" rather than "different kinds of success." The recap layer has no distinct destiny sentence or punchline per route — players leave with "I became a merchant" rather than "I became this specific kind of merchant in this specific way."

The thinnest spots:
1. **Success shape at payoff** — All three read as "you became a successful merchant with [X] background decoration" rather than "you succeeded in a fundamentally different way"
2. **Recap / destiny sentence** — No route-specific closing punchline that players can walk away remembering
3. **Success-shape structural markers** — No flags or carriers that differentiate *how* success was achieved beyond origin flavor

## 2. Success-Shape Signal Inventory by Route

### 2.1 Apprentice Route (town_apprentice → apprentice_merchant_bridge_crossed)

**Bridge layer (P58):**
- **Success seed:** Partnership with trade contacts — "从学徒踏上了商路"
- **Success shape narrative:** Craft skill → trade curiosity → partnership → business expansion
- **Core metaphor:** Business as an extension of craft mastery
- **Memory:** "你与买卖人合伙经商，从学徒踏上了商路。"
- **Summary:** "学徒出身的商人：从铺子学徒到商路合伙，跨越了手艺与买卖的界限。"

**Entry layer (P63 — magnate_on_ramp):**
- **Current goal:** "手艺学透、合伙商路已通，正谋划更大的局面"
- **Cost label:** "手艺与合伙的担子"
- **Age-40 identity:** "手艺为基，合伙为径，商路是技能延伸的版图"

**Pressure layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_midlife_pressure):**
- **Current goal:** "商号遍九州，合伙人与账目债也遍九州，供货的账期、销路的分成拴住了手艺人的手脚"
- **Success-through lens:** Success through judgment, craft quality, and partnership trust
- **Distinctive keywords:** 合伙人, 账目, 供货, 账期, 销路, 分成, 手艺人

**Payoff layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_payoff):**
- **Current goal:** "巨贾之位到手，供货销路尽在掌握，只是当年的手艺人如今要看合伙人的脸色，账目上的分成比刨子上的木纹更难拿捏"
- **Success shape as expressed:** "巨贾之位到手" + cost reflection
- **What's strong:** The "just... but" pivot (只是) creates cost tension
- **What's still generic:** The success itself is still "巨贾之位" — the same destination as the other two routes, just with different scenery
- **No "apprentice-shaped success" marker** — nothing that says "you succeeded *by* being a craftsperson-turned-partner" rather than "you succeeded *and* you used to be a craftsperson"

**What's missing for success-shape differentiation:**
- No marker that the success was achieved *through* craft judgment and partnership (as opposed to achieved *despite* or *alongside*)
- No recap punchline that distills the apprentice journey into one memorable sentence
- The success shape is additive ("you're a merchant AND you have craft background") rather than transformative ("you succeeded in a craft-partnership way")

- **Summary:** "酒肆出身的商人：从跑堂伙计到城里铺子，靠人脉铺出了商路。"

**Entry layer (P63 — magnate_on_ramp):**

- **Cost label:** "人脉与铺子的担子"

**Pressure layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_midlife_pressure):**

- **Success-through lens:** Success through contacts, recommendations, and information flow
- **Distinctive keywords:** 人情, 面子, 老主顾, 欠情, 人脉, 引荐

**Payoff layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_payoff):**

- **What's strong:** "凭人脉通八方" is more route-specific than generic "巨贾之位"

**What's missing for success-shape differentiation:**
- No marker that success is fundamentally network-shaped (information broker, connector, relationship manager)
- No destiny sentence that captures "you succeeded by knowing everyone and owing everyone"
- The success is still framed as "merchant success with network decoration" rather than "network success that happens to be in merchant form"

### 2.3 Peasant Route (farm_peasant → peasant_merchant_bridge_crossed)

**Bridge layer (P61):**
- **Success seed:** Grain-trade labor → cargo handling → small-scale trading — "从田间走到粮路上"
- **Success shape narrative:** Physical labor → grain route work → logistics understanding → scale through endurance
- **Core metaphor:** Business as earned through body and persistence
- **Memory:** "你从田间走到粮路上，从帮工做起，渐渐摸通了粮货买卖。"
- **Summary:** "农家出身的粮货商人：从田埂到粮路，靠体力和勤恳踏出生意路。"

**Entry layer (P63 — magnate_on_ramp):**
- **Current goal:** "粮路跑通、买卖上手，正学着像商人一样思考"
- **Cost label:** "粮路与买卖的担子"
- **Age-40 identity:** "力气为基，跑商为径，商路是勤劳致富的通道"

**Pressure layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_midlife_pressure):**
- **Current goal:** "商号遍九州，车马仓储债也遍九州，运力、仓库、下属工钱让泥腿子巨贾用身体在扛"
- **Success-through lens:** Success through physical endurance, cargo risk, and hard-won scale
- **Distinctive keywords:** 车马, 仓储, 运力, 仓库, 工钱, 泥腿子, 跑商

**Payoff layer (P64 + P66 — magnate_payoff):**
- **Current goal:** "车马仓储物流根基已成，泥腿子熬出了头，只是脚下的路比田埂还长，每一步都赌过收成、押过季节，赢了但也再回不到田里了"
- **Success shape as expressed:** "车马仓储物流根基已成，泥腿子熬出了头" + cost reflection
- **What's strong:** "熬出了头" (endured your way to the top) has a more distinctive success flavor than the other two
- **What's still generic:** Even with "熬出了头", the final shape is still "you became a merchant magnate" — the peasant-ness is in the path, not in the shape of the success itself
- **Peasant success is closest to distinct** but still not shaped differently enough

**What's missing for success-shape differentiation:**
- No marker that the success is fundamentally *logistics-shaped* or *labor-built* rather than just "merchant success with peasant flavor"
- No destiny sentence that captures "you bet everything with your body and won"
- "勤劳致富" in the identity is the most generic phrase across all three routes

### 3.2 What Makes a Good Destiny Sentence

A good destiny sentence:
1. **Is memorable** — short enough to repeat, vivid enough to stick
2. **Is route-distinct** — you can tell which route it came from without context
3. **Captures both success and cost** — it's not just "you won" or "you lost", it's "you won this specific thing and paid this specific price"
4. **Shapes the success** — it describes *what kind* of success, not just *that* you succeeded
5. **Echoes the origin** — you can hear the starting point in the ending

## 4. Success-Shape Differentiation: Where We Are vs. Where We Could Be

### 4.1 Current State: Flavor Differentiation

Right now, the trilogy is at **flavor-level differentiation**:
- Same success shape (巨贾 / merchant magnate)
- Different origin decoration (apprentice / tavern / peasant background)
- Different cost decoration (different kinds of burdens)
- Same structural arc (on_ramp → pressure → payoff)

### 4.2 Target State (for P67): Shape-Level Differentiation (Bounded)

Within bounded scope (no new systems, no new events, no structural change), P67 can push toward **shape-level differentiation in expression**:
- Same structural skeleton, but success reads as *qualitatively different*
- Each route has a distinct success metaphor that's more than decoration
- Each route has a destiny sentence that captures its specific shape
- Markers/carriers differentiate "what kind of success" not just "where you came from"

### 4.3 What Shape Differentiation Could Look Like (Expression-Only)

| Route | Success Shape (Metaphor) | Destiny Sentence Direction |
|-------|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Apprentice** | Success through **judgment and craft quality** — the merchant who succeeds by knowing what's good | "从刨子到账本，靠手艺的眼光算出了一片商路" |
| **Tavern** | Success through **connections and information flow** — the merchant who succeeds by knowing everyone | "从酒肆到商号，靠人情的网络织出了八方商路" |
| **Peasant** | Success through **endurance and cargo risk** — the merchant who succeeds by out-working and out-lasting everyone | "从田埂到车马，靠脚力和血汗踩出了一条粮路" |

Key difference from current state: The success itself is shaped by the origin path, not just decorated by it.

## 5. Thinnest Spots (P67 Targets)

### 5.1 #1: Success-Shape Metaphor at Payoff

The payoff phase currently frames success as "巨贾之位" (magnate position) with route flavor. What's missing is a route-specific success metaphor that makes the *shape* of success feel different:

- **Apprentice gap:** Success feels like "you're a magnate who used to be an apprentice" rather than "you're a craft-judgment magnate"
- **Tavern gap:** Success feels like "you're a magnate who used to be a tavern hand" rather than "you're a network-broker magnate"
- **Peasant gap:** Success feels like "you're a magnate who used to be a peasant" rather than "you're a logistics-endurance magnate"

### 5.2 #2: Recap / Destiny Sentence

There's no single punchline sentence per route that players can walk away remembering. The age-40 identity is closest, but it's:
- Too long to be a "destiny sentence"
- Framed as identity ("你是...的巨贾") rather than as a story arc summary
- Missing the "success shape" punch — it describes the path more than the shape of what was built

### 5.3 #3: Success-Shape Marker / Carrier

There are flags for "which bridge you crossed" (apprentice_merchant_bridge_crossed, etc.) but no flags or markers for "what kind of success you achieved." The differentiation is entirely through the origin lens, not through a success-shape lens. For recap and expression purposes, having a marker that says "this is a [craft/network/logistics]-shaped success" would make the differentiation more robust.

## 6. What P67 Can Do (Within Bounded Scope)

P67 can strengthen success-shape and recap differentiation **without new systems or events** by:

1. **Reframing payoff success metaphors** — shift from "you became a magnate with [X] background" to "you built [X]-shaped success"
2. **Adding destiny-sentence / recap-line expression** — create a short, memorable closing punchline per route on existing expression surfaces
3. **Strengthening age-40 identity with success shape** — make the identity emphasize *what kind* of success, not just *where you came from*
4. **Adding success-shape markers** (lightweight flags or derived markers) so expressions can reference the shape of success, not just the origin
5. **Refining cost-label alignment** — make the cost label echo the success shape (cost of craft-judgment success, cost of network success, cost of logistics success)

---

To stay in bounded scope:

- ❌ No structural change to the magnate skeleton
- ❌ No mechanical differentiation of success

## 9. Conclusion

The merchant trilogy has **strong entry differentiation** and **strong cost differentiation**, but the **success shape** still converges to "you became a merchant magnate" with origin decoration. P67's opportunity is to make the success itself feel shaped differently — not just the path to it, and not just the cost of it.

The highest-leverage targets, in order:
1. **Success-shape metaphor at payoff** — reframe "巨贾之位" to route-specific success shapes
2. **Destiny sentence / recap line** — add a short, memorable closing punchline per route
3. **Success-shape markers** — lightweight carriers that let expressions reference the shape of success

All three can be done within the existing expression/marker framework — no new systems needed. And because P66 already made the cost felt throughout the journey, the success-shape differentiation and recap punchline will land much harder than they would have before.
